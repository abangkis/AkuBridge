    About AkuBrowser

    A concise feed, processed on your device

    Overview

    AkuBrowser reads bounded samples from the feeds you choose, organizes them into a local timeline, and uses Codex to help identify relevant updates.

    AB

    AkuBrowser Extension

    AkuBridge reads feeds in read-only mode only after you grant access.

    AS

    AkuSidecar

    The local runtime that stores data and builds your private timeline.

    C2

    C2PA Verification

    Checks image provenance information locally when it is available.

    CX

    Codex App

    The AI engine you install and sign in to for reasoning features.
